HD textures of the Ultra graphics (data/hd/*.jpg)

The albedo images were generated with FLUX.1 [schnell] (black-forest-labs/flux-schnell),
released by Black Forest Labs under the Apache License 2.0:
https://github.com/black-forest-labs/flux/blob/main/model_licenses/LICENSE-FLUX1-schnell
The normal, roughness and occlusion maps are derived from them by tools/hd_textures.py.
The files, their prompts and seeds are listed in data/hd/README.md.
