Edifice Readme (alpha version .1a)
a game created from scratch by Particle and Nelso

Getting Started:

Ok, you've extracted Edifice into a folder and now you've launched the readme.  Before running the game, you need to edit a few INI files in order to set your controls, preferences, name, and all that other good stuff.

Start off by opening net.ini and look at the fourth line where it says "name=Player".  Change the word "Player" to whatever you want your alias to be in the game.

The second and last file you need to edit is control.ini.  Editing this file is essential.  Instructions for editing are enclosed in the file.

w00t! Can I run the game now??

Yes, but there are a few things you should know first:
This game was produced on NVIDIA cards.  Everything should be perfect as long as you have a TNT or better graphics chipset.  Unfortunately, we can't say the same for voodoos.  A few of our testers have had luck with voodoo3 cards, but the ground textures will look like crap.  This is caused by the 256x256 texture limitation of voodoo3 and below 3DFX cards.  The game hasn't been tested on the new voodoo5 cards yet, so no word on whether that works or not.

If you have a bad graphic card like the old ATI Rage Pro, then you're out of luck.  Go buy a TNT or something.

The minimum CPU speed is 266 pentium II.  We recommend at least a 350 or 400 MHz pentium II or III though.  Any athlon should rox0r too.

Ok, try running Edifice.exe!

We're gonna assume that it works.  Try running around a bit to get a decent feel for the game.  Once you're done, refer back to this guide for some tips.

I'm back!

You probably just ran around in a single player game with nothing to do.  Now's the time to find a friend or a server to play on.

The person that hits the "server" button on the startup screen is the server.  All other players should hit the "play" button and enter the IP of the server in the box, then hit refresh.  Once the server name appears, give it a click and hit the join button.

Once 2 or more players are in the game, the server will automatically assign teams.  The first player on each team gets the base station.  A message that says "Place this somewhere good" will appear on the screen.  Find a strategic point on the map and hit your claim button.  This will drop the station.

What's a station?

The station is the centerpiece of your team.  You can only build structures within a certain radius of it, and it's also the point where the other team can capture your flag.

If you come across the other team's station, walk over it and hit your claim button. This will grab a flag.  A message will tell you to take it back to your station.  Once you arrive at your base station, hit the claim button to finish capturing the flag.  Now your team has a point!

What's the deal with stations?

Defending your station is the key.  Building structures around it makes it harder for the other team to capture your flags.

Wait: What's the deal with buildings?

Buildings are a crucial part of edifice.  Bring up your build menu by hitting the build key (default is B)

A list of buildings will pop up in the center of your screen.  Navigate these choices with the number keys on your keyboard.  Once you select a building you want, hit its number and then "1" to build it.

w00t! a building!

Isn't that rox0r?

Yeah!

Building a nice base is an important aspect of Edifice.  Don't think about building a chunk of blocks over your station to prevent the other team from capturing.  If you do this, then you won't be able to capture flags since you need to be able to access your station.  If ruining games is your thing, then don't play Edifice.  The game is easy to mess with in its current state and strict server-enforced rules are a goal for future versions.

How do I win?

The current capturelimit is 10, meaning that once a team gets 10 flag returns, they win the game.  Once the game is won, the land will be randomly generated again and everything will be reset.

Oh yeah, what's the deal with weapons?

You start out with the Beretta pistol, UMP submachine gun, and Grenades.  All ammo is currently unlimited, but reload times are high as a compromise.

Other weapons are randomly awarded to team members for successfully capturing flags and other stuff like that.

Oh yeah, what's the deal with hang gliders?

Jump off a mountain or something, then press and hold your hangglide button.  You're flying now.
